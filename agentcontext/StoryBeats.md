# Story beats 
General story arc of the game 

Week numbers follow Calendar.md, and the subject pacing there (about 2 to 3 generic subjects a week). Locations are from Setting.md. "Generic N" is a subject from the general curriculum (1 to 33). "Jay/Monty/Ciel/Julian Ch N" is a special chapter from that character's route table. Each special chapter needs its generic subjects and the character's previous chapter done, so the weeks below are the earliest a player on pace would see it.

## Lesson unlocks by week
Roughly what a player on pace can unlock each week. Generic subjects open in order, about 2 to 3 a week. Finishing a subject with any character opens the next one for everyone, and earlier subjects can still be done with other characters. A special chapter opens once its generic subjects and that character's previous chapter are done. Players who fall behind unlock things later, and nothing locks again once it's open.

Weeks 5, 7 and 10 are light on special chapters, so they're good weeks for recurring events, birthday events, and catching up on affection.

### 1. Orientation
Welcome to Runtime university! 
- Player recieves Quack Overflow, and a map of the university 
- Week 1, Monday, on the main quad. The player names the heroine (default Grace) and answers "Why did you pick CS?" (games, money, curiosity, "I panicked").
- Quack claims today is their birthday ("I'm one day old. Where's my cake?").

### 2. Campus Tour: Meet the love interests 
Go on a tour of campus (Led by Quack Overflow) and "coincidentally" meet each of the love interests
The meet involves completing lesson one (Printing to the console) with each of them. 
Quack explains how lessons work and how more lessons get unlocks. Quack also explains how lessons add affection (Quack explains affection too), and can unlocking special lessons too. 
- Week 1. Tour stops, in order:
    - **Main quad help desk:** Monty walks her through print("Hello, world!").
    - **Turing Hall, TA office hours:** Day Jay corrects her console.log before she finishes typing it.
    - **Student council room:** Julian "drafts" her into paperwork and explains every word of public static void main.
    - **Hopper Computer Lab (last stop, after dark):** Ciel insults her Hello World, then quietly fixes the #include.
- Generic 1 (Printing to the console) is done four times here, once per character.

### 3. Club fair and the weekly schedule
- Week 1, Friday, on the main quad. Generic 2 (Comments) is available this week.
- Each love interest has a booth or a reason to be there, and each one introduces their two recurring events (see Calendar.md): office hours and hackathon nights for Jay, gardening club and tutoring shifts for Monty, game dev club and midnight lab sessions for Ciel, and council meetings and the coffee society for Julian.
- Quack explains Day and Night slots, the weekly calendar, and that Jay seems to have "two completely different schedules".

### 4. The Day 256 Hackathon
- Week 2, Saturday to Sunday night, in the student union hackathon hall. It's held on Programmers' Day (September 13).
- Jay Ch1 (Variables, night): the heroine meets a wild hacker in a yellow hoodie who teaches over pizza, and has no idea it's the strict TA from office hours.
- Monday after, Day Jay finds 2 a.m. texts to the heroine and a hackathon trophy they don't remember winning.
- Also this week: Monty Ch1 (The Zen of Python, tutoring center) and Julian Ch1 (The JVM, council room and server room).

### 5. First problem set and the reveal
- Week 3. The first problem set is due, and the tutoring center opens its evening hours.
- Jay Ch2 (Type annotations, day): Day Jay types the same code from the hackathon. At sunset on the student union rooftop, the glasses slip off and the palette goes from blue to yellow. The reveal: it's the same person.
- Also this week: Ciel Ch1 (Compiling and types, Hopper Lab) and Julian Ch2 (== vs .equals(), council room).

### 6. Quack's check-in and the hidden Polyglot choice
- Week 4, end of the week. Quack gives a full affection report in the heroine's dorm room. No routes close: every character stays available all semester.
- Replay only: if all four are at 20+ and the player has seen at least one good ending, a hidden choice appears: "Why choose just one language?" Picking it starts the Polyglot route (see below). Quack: "You absolute multithreader."

### 7. The fall carnival
- Weeks 5 and 6, weekend nights on the athletic field.
- Jay Ch3 (Equality and coercion, night): a carnival date full of rigged games. Teaches == vs === and why '5' + 3 is '53'.
- Generic 10 (Logical operators) can be played here: "must be this tall AND have a ticket".
- Hacktoberfest starts in week 6, which gives Jay and Ciel something to argue about.
- Also in week 6: Monty Ch2 (Slicing and unpacking, Café Cache), Monty Ch3 (Comprehensions, picnic lawn), Ciel Ch2 (Pointers and references, Hopper Lab), and Julian Ch3 (Primitives and wrappers, Café Cache).

### 8. Midterms and Ciel's birthday
- Week 7. Midterms cover subjects 1 to 17, with one exam scene in the exam hall.
- Tuesday, October 13: Ada Lovelace Day talk in Lovelace Library. Every character has a different opinion of it.
- Wednesday, October 14: **Ciel's birthday.** Ciel spends it alone in the lab, assuming nobody knows. If the heroine brings black coffee or a new keycap at night, it's a big affection event ("I-it's not like I wanted a cake").

### 9. After midterms
- Week 8. Grades come back, and Quack reacts to the heroine's score.
- Jay Ch4 (Interfaces, day, office hours): Day Jay writes an interface for "the ideal partner" and it describes the heroine.
- Jay Ch5 (Closures and the DOM, night, hackathon hall): Jay volunteers to build the end-of-term festival site. Night Jay remembers every detail from the night they met.
- Monty Ch4 (Duck typing, duck pond): Quack's starring role.
- Julian Ch4 (Null and Optional, University Archive): the missing portrait in the Duke family archive.

### 10. Halloween and Fright Night
- Week 9. Halloween (Saturday, October 31) costume party at the student union, and the carnival returns as Fright Night (Friday and Saturday nights in weeks 9 and 10).
- Generic 24 (Linked lists: ride cars hooked together) and 26 (Recursion: the hall of mirrors) are played at Fright Night.
- Week 9 specials: Jay Ch6 (Async and promises, dusk, student union rooftop), Monty Ch5 (Context managers, greenhouse), Ciel Ch3 (Memory management, Hopper Lab), Ciel Ch4 (RAII and smart pointers, game dev club room), and Julian Ch5 (Checked exceptions, council room).
- Week 10: spring course registration opens, and everyone asks the heroine which classes she'll take next semester. Ciel Ch5 (Classes and the STL, Hopper Lab).

### 11. Festival planning begins
- Week 11. The end-of-term festival and the winter gala are announced. Jay's festival site gets serious, and the council starts the gala guest list.
- **Jay Ch7 (final challenge, dusk, Lovelace Library):** convert the festival site from JS to TS together, one type at a time, as the sun sets under the library lights. Jay's is the first route final.

### 12. Final project crunch
- Week 12. Everyone is stressed, and the dining hall is open late.
- **Monty Ch6 (final challenge, campus garden and hedge maze):** exploring the garden paths with `yield`, one step at a time, then a decorator that adds a heart to every flower.

### 13. Thanksgiving break
- Week 13. Campus goes quiet from Thursday to Sunday, and the dining hall runs short hours. Characters who stay on campus are easier to find alone.
- Monday, November 23: Julian's "half-birthday", observed formally "for scheduling reasons", with a printed agenda.
- **Julian Ch6 (final challenge, Oracle Great Hall):** planning the winter gala with generics and the Streams API. Julian visibly loosens up.

### 14. Finals week and confessions
- Week 14. Generic 33 (Dynamic programming) is the final exam, in the exam hall.
- **Ciel Ch6 (final challenge, Hopper Lab):** an all-night Templates duet, the hardest challenge in the game. Ciel confesses at dawn on the CS building rooftop, coat around her shoulders.
- **Thursday, the winter gala (Oracle Great Hall):** Julian confesses on the council room balcony afterward, tie loosened for the first time.
- **Friday, December 4: Jay's birthday and the end-of-term festival** on the main quad, running on Jay's site. Night Jay throws a party in the group chat, and Day Jay insists it's "just a normal day". Jay confesses on the festival stage at sunset, both sides at once. Monty confesses under the greenhouse lights during the festival.
- Only the route the player is on confesses. The ending depends on affection: Good is 80+ with the final challenge passed, Normal is 50 to 79, and Bad is under 50.

### 15. Endings and epilogue
- Each route has a good, normal, and bad ending (Jay has two normal endings, day and night). See Characters.md for each one.
- Epilogue scenes can use the birthdays outside the semester: Monty's (February 20) in the spring, Julian's real birthday (May 23), and the heroine's (default December 9, Grace Hopper's birthday).
- Seeing all four good endings unlocks the secret route with Ferris Oxide (Rust).

## Polyglot route (replay only)
Runs alongside the calendar above, starting from the hidden choice at the end of week 4. All four must reach 80+ by week 14, and Jay's day and night lesson counts must be within 2 of each other (see Balance.md).
- **Weeks 5 to 8, rivalry:** all four notice they're being "juggled" and start competing. The jealousy scenes are Julian versus Ciel, Monty versus Jay, and Day Jay versus Night Jay. Carnival nights and Ciel's birthday become competitions too.
- **Weeks 9 to 11, the capstone:** in the capstone team room, a professor assigns the heroine a team project with all four. They build a dating sim: Python for the AI, Julian on the server, Ciel on the engine, and Jay on the front end.
- **Week 12, the merge conflict:** everyone pushes at once and the project breaks. They argue about whose language should be "main", and the group splits up.
- **Week 13, resolving it:** the quiet Thanksgiving campus. The heroine visits each one alone at their usual spot and resolves their part of the conflict.
- **Week 14, the integration test:** one short problem in each language, with the matching character beside her. Then the rooftop confession at dusk on the student union roof: "I commit to all of you."
- **Endings:** the Polyglot ending (all 80+, epilogue CG of all five on the rooftop at sunset, with a post-credits tease of Ferris) or the merge conflict ending (everyone walks away at the festival, and the heroine ends up on the quad fountain bench with Quack: "So. You and me, then.").
