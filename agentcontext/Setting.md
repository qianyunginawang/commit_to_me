# Setting: 
Runtime University, one fall semester (14 weeks). The heroine is a CS freshman. Each language is an upperclassman who tutors her in their own features, and every tutorial chapter doubles as a date.


## Runtime University features: 
Campus locations, grouped by area. Each one lists the dates that use it. "Generic N" is a subject from the general curriculum (1 to 33), and "Jay/Monty/Ciel/Julian Ch N" is a special chapter from that character's route table. Locations with no special chapter can still be picked for generic dates with anyone.

### Academic buildings
- **Turing Hall (lecture building):** big lecture halls for Intro to Programming, where Day Jay is the TA. Hallways lined with numbered lockers.
    - Generic 3 (Variables and assignment), 9 (If/else), 16 (Arrays and lists: a row of numbered lockers)
- **TA office hours room (in Turing Hall):** a small, very tidy room with a whiteboard. Day slot only.
    - Jay Ch2 (Type annotations), Jay Ch4 (Interfaces)
- **Hopper Computer Lab (basement of the CS building):** rows of humming machines, flickering lights, always open. Ciel practically lives here at 2 a.m.
    - Generic 8 (Debugging)
    - Ciel Ch1 (Compiling and types: where the heroine stumbles in late at night), Ciel Ch2 (Pointers and references), Ciel Ch3 (Memory management), Ciel Ch5 (Classes and the STL), Ciel Ch6 (Templates final, an all-nighter)
    - Ciel normal ending (the lab door left unlocked for her), Ciel good ending CG (both asleep on the keyboard)
- **CS building rooftop:** reached through a fire door at the top of the lab stairwell. Quiet, cold, with a view of the sunrise.
    - Ciel confession (at dawn after the Templates all-nighter, coat around her shoulders)
- **Capstone team room (CS building, 3rd floor):** a glass-walled project room with a big screen for code review.
    - Polyglot route weeks 9 to 12 (the capstone project and the merge conflict), week 14 (the integration test)
- **Exam hall:** rows of single desks, a big clock on the wall. Used for midterms and finals week.
    - Generic 33 (Dynamic programming: a finals-week cram session)
- **Tutoring center:** couches, snacks, and a whiteboard covered in other people's handwriting. Monty volunteers here.
    - Generic 12 (For loops)
    - Monty Ch1 (The Zen of Python: Monty types `import this` into the REPL)

### Library and archives
- **Lovelace Library:** tall shelves, reading lamps, and a card catalog nobody uses but Monty. Day slot, plus late nights during finals.
    - Generic 17 (Maps and dictionaries: the catalog maps call numbers to books), 31 (Searching), 32 (Sorting: reshelving returns)
    - Jay Ch7 (Final challenge at dusk: working under the library lights as the sun sets)
- **University Archive (Sun Wing):** dusty records and old portraits of the Sun household, which the Oracle family later absorbed. Julian has a key.
    - Generic 21 (Inheritance and polymorphism: family-tree portraits), 23 (File reading and writing: digging through old records)
    - Julian Ch4 (Null and Optional: the missing portrait in the Duke family archive)

### Student government
- **Student council room:** labeled folders, two coffee mugs, a very large binder. Julian is always here.
    - Julian Ch1 (The JVM: the heroine is "drafted" for council paperwork), Julian Ch2 (== vs .equals(): two identical forms), Julian Ch5 (Checked exceptions)
    - Julian good ending CG (two mugs on the council desk)
- **Council room balcony:** looks over the main quad.
    - Julian confession (after the winter gala, tie loosened for the first time)
- **Council server room (admin basement):** ancient, humming servers that have run campus systems forever.
    - Julian Ch1 (The JVM: the server room tour, "write once, run anywhere")
- **Oracle Great Hall:** the formal ballroom, donated by the Oracle family. Chandeliers and a long guest list.
    - Julian Ch6 (Generics and streams final: planning the winter gala)

### Student union
- **Student union hackathon hall:** a big open room with power strips everywhere, pizza boxes, and LED strips. Night slot. Doubles as the makerspace.
    - Generic 22 (Error handling), 30 (Big-O basics: racing a slow solution against a fast one)
    - Jay Ch1 (Variables, night: the hackathon where the heroine meets a wild hacker over pizza), Jay Ch5 (Closures and the DOM: building the campus festival site)
    - Jay good ending (launching the site they built together)
- **Student union rooftop:** string lights, a couple of beat-up beanbags, and the best sunset on campus. Night Jay's favorite spot ("We're going to the roof").
    - Jay Ch2 ending (the heroine watches the sunset switch happen: the reveal), Jay Ch6 (Async and promises, dusk)
    - Polyglot confession (at dusk, so both sides of Jay are there), Polyglot ending epilogue CG (all five at sunset, Quack on her shoulder)
- **Game dev club room:** a cramped room off the union with old consoles and a whiteboard of engine diagrams. Not officially a club. Ciel runs it from the shadows.
    - Ciel Ch4 (RAII and smart pointers: Ciel admits they don't have to manage everything alone)

### Food and shops

- **Fitness center and running track:**
