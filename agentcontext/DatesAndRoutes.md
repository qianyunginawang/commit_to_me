# Character specific
Specific dates where the player learns about particular features of the language
## Javascript/Typescript 
Route outline.
Night chapters teach a JavaScript feature, and the next day chapter adds TypeScript on top of it.
1. Meeting (common route): two meetings that seem unrelated. A strict TA at daytime office hours, then a wild hacker at a night hackathon who teaches console.log over pizza. The heroine doesn't realize they're the same person.
2. Chapter 1, Variables (night): Jay admits a messy past with var. Teaches let, const, and why var is trouble.
3. Chapter 2, Type annotations (day): the TA adds types to the same code. Teaches string, number, and boolean. At the end, the heroine watches the sunset switch happen. The reveal.
4. Chapter 3, Equality and coercion (night): a carnival date full of weird games. Teaches == versus === and why '5' + 3 is '53'.
5. Chapter 4, Interfaces (day): Day Jay, flustered, writes an interface for "the ideal partner" and it describes the heroine. Teaches interfaces and typed function parameters.
6. Chapter 5, Closures and the DOM (night): building the campus festival site. Night Jay remembers every detail from the night they met. Teaches arrow functions, closures, and event listeners.
7. Mid-route conflict: someone mocks the festival site as "spaghetti code". Day Jay tries to erase the night side for good, staying up under the library lights to avoid sunset.
8. Chapter 6, Async and promises (dusk): the heroine gets both sides to await each other instead of fighting. Teaches promises and async/await.
9. Final challenge (dusk): convert a JavaScript file to TypeScript together, one type at a time. Gradual typing as a metaphor for accepting both halves.
10. Confession: on the festival stage at sunset, glasses pushed up into their hair, both sides at once.
Endings.
• Good (affection 80+, both meters balanced): Jay stops hiding the switch and both halves share memories. Epilogue CG: Jay at dusk in the yellow hoodie and the blue glasses, launching a site they built with the heroine.
• Normal, day (Day affection much higher): Jay stays TypeScript full-time. Polite, proper, and a little hollow. The 2 a.m. texts stop.
• Normal, night (Night affection much higher): JavaScript takes over. Jay quits the TA job and is always fun, but never quite there when it matters.
• Bad: "undefined." Jay waves at the heroine, then can't remember her name, day or night.

## Python 
Route outline.
1. Meeting (common route): Monty runs the orientation-day help desk and walks the heroine through print("Hello, world!").
2. Chapter 1, Variables and types: a garden walk. Labeling plant pots teaches variables, strings, and numbers.
3. Chapter 2, Indentation and control flow: a study session where Monty gets adorably fussy. Teaches if/elif/else and for loops.
4. Chapter 3, Lists and dictionaries: packing a picnic basket together. Teaches lists, dicts, and list comprehensions.
5. Chapter 4, Functions: Monty writes the heroine a function that "returns a compliment". Teaches def, parameters, and return values.
6. Mid-route conflict: an old Python 2 friend shows up and asks Monty to "go back to how things were". Monty pulls away.
7. Chapter 5, Exceptions: the heroine helps Monty write try/except. The lesson and the reconciliation are the same: "It's okay to fail. You just have to catch it."
8. Chapter 6, Classes: building a small Garden class together. Final challenge.
9. Confession: under the greenhouse lights at the end-of-term festival.
Endings.
• Good: Monty asks the heroine to co-author their senior research project. Epilogue CG: the two of them in the greenhouse, a plant labeled with her name.
• Normal: they become close study partners, with a hint that something more could happen "in the next version".
• Bad: "DeprecationWarning." Monty stays kind but distant, and the heroine sees them tutoring someone else.


## C++ 
Route outline.
1. Meeting (common route): the heroine stumbles into the lab late at night. Ciel insults her Hello World, then quietly fixes the #include.
2. Chapter 1, Compiling and types: Ciel makes her compile and run code by hand. Teaches main, std::cout, and why types matter for memory.
3. Chapter 2, Pointers and references: the infamous lesson. Teaches *, &, and addresses, with the reference line as a foreshadowed confession.
4. Chapter 3, Memory management: a late-night debugging session that turns into a memory leak disaster. Teaches new, delete, and stack versus heap.
5. Chapter 4, RAII and smart pointers: Ciel admits they don't have to manage everything alone. Teaches destructors, std::unique_ptr ("exclusive"), and std::shared_ptr.
6. Mid-route conflict: a visiting researcher says Ciel should be replaced with a "safer" language. Ciel shuts the heroine out completely.
7. Chapter 5, Classes and the STL: the heroine breaks through by using Ciel's own tools. Teaches classes, std::vector, and std::string.
8. Chapter 6, Templates: Ciel's hardest and proudest feature, taught as a duet. Final challenge, the hardest in the game.
9. Confession: on the lab roof at dawn after an all-nighter, coat around her shoulders.
Endings.
• Good: Ciel lets the heroine contribute to their life's work, a game engine, and puts her name in the credits. Epilogue CG: both asleep on the keyboard as the build finishes.
• Normal: Ciel admits she's "not completely incompetent" and leaves the lab door unlocked for her.
• Bad: "Segmentation fault (core dumped)." The route crashes to a black screen, then Ciel walks past without recognizing her. Undefined behavior.

## Java 
Route outline.
1. Meeting (common route): the heroine is "drafted" to help with council paperwork. Julian walks her through public static void main.
2. Chapter 1, Types and variables: filling out council forms correctly. Teaches primitive types and strict declarations.
3. Chapter 2, Classes and objects: organizing the club fair. Each club is an object. Teaches classes, constructors, and new.
4. Chapter 3, Encapsulation: Julian keeps their feelings private and only shares them through "approved getters". Teaches access modifiers, getters, and setters.
5. Chapter 4, Inheritance and interfaces: a visit to the Duke family archive. Teaches extends, implements, and abstract classes.
6. Mid-route conflict: a council vote to replace Julian with a "more modern" system. Julian hides it and tries to handle everything alone.
7. Chapter 5, Exceptions: the heroine teaches Julian that declaring risks with throws is honesty, not weakness. Checked exceptions and try/catch.
8. Chapter 6, Collections and lambdas: planning the winter gala. Teaches ArrayList, HashMap, and lambdas, which is Julian visibly loosening up. Final challenge.
9. Confession: on the council room balcony after the gala, tie loosened for the first time.
Endings.
• Good: Julian keeps the presidency on their own terms and asks the heroine to be vice president, and partner. Epilogue CG: two mugs on the council desk.
• Normal: Julian names her their most trusted council member and promises "a formal proposal in the next release".
• Bad: "NullPointerException." Heroine reaches for his hand and finds nothing there.
