# General 
The general curriculum. Every subject can be taken with any of the four characters, in that character's language (TypeScript with Day Jay, JavaScript with Night Jay). Subjects unlock in order. Generic 1 is done with all four characters on the campus tour.

#### Basics:
1. Printing to the console
2. Comments
3. Variables and assignment
4. Numbers and math
5. Strings
6. Booleans and comparisons
7. User input
8. Debugging

#### Control flow:
9. If/else
10. Logical operators
11. While loops
12. For loops
13. Nested loops and break/continue

#### Organizing code:
14. Functions
15. Scope

#### Built-in collections:
16. Arrays and lists
17. Maps and dictionaries
18. Sets
19. Iterating over collections

#### Bigger ideas:
20. Classes and objects
21. Inheritance and polymorphism
22. Error handling
23. File reading and writing

#### Data structures:
24. Linked lists
25. Stacks and queues

#### Intro algorithms:
30. Big-O basics

32. Sorting
33. Dynamic programming

# Character specific
Specific dates where the player learns about particular features of the language. A special chapter unlocks once its generic subjects and the character's previous chapter are done.

## Javascript/Typescript 
Night chapters teach a JavaScript feature, and day chapters add TypeScript on top of it.

| # | Special chapter | Generic unlock | Generic chapter name | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Variables (night) | 3 | Variables and assignment | The heroine meets a wild hacker at a night hackathon, not realizing it's the strict TA from office hours. Over pizza, Jay admits a messy past with var. Teaches let, const, and why var is trouble. |
| 2 | Type annotations (day) | 4, 5, 6 | Numbers and math; Strings; Booleans and comparisons | The TA adds types to the same code. Teaches string, number, and boolean. Ends with the sunset switch: the reveal. |
| 3 | Equality and coercion (night) | 6, 9 | Booleans and comparisons; If/else | A carnival date full of weird games. Teaches == vs === and why '5' + 3 is '53'. |
| 4 | Interfaces (day) | 14, 20 | Functions; Classes and objects | A flustered Day Jay writes an interface for "the ideal partner" and it describes the heroine. Teaches interfaces and typed parameters. |
| 5 | Closures and the DOM (night) | 15 | Scope | Building the campus festival site. Night Jay remembers every detail from the night they met. Teaches arrow functions, closures, and event listeners. |
| 6 | Async and promises (dusk) | 22, 23 | Error handling; File reading and writing | The heroine gets both sides to await each other instead of fighting. Teaches promises and async/await. |
| 7 | Final challenge (dusk) | 27 | Trees | Convert the festival site from JS to TS together, one type at a time, walking the DOM tree. Gradual typing as accepting both halves. |

### Confession
On the festival stage at sunset, glasses pushed up into their hair, both sides at once.

### Endings
• Good (affection 80+, both meters balanced): Jay stops hiding the switch and both halves share memories. Epilogue CG: Jay at dusk in the yellow hoodie and the blue glasses, launching a site they built with the heroine.
• Normal, day (Day affection much higher): Jay stays TypeScript full-time. Polite, proper, and a little hollow. The 2 a.m. texts stop.
• Normal, night (Night affection much higher): JavaScript takes over. Jay quits the TA job and is always fun, but never quite there when it matters.
• Bad: "undefined." Jay waves at the heroine, then can't remember her name, day or night.

Under the greenhouse lights at the end-of-term festival.

### Endings
• Good: Monty asks the heroine to co-author their senior research project. Epilogue CG: the two of them in the greenhouse, a plant labeled with her name.

• Bad: "DeprecationWarning." Monty stays kind but distant, and the heroine sees them tutoring someone else.

## C++ 
| # | Special chapter | Generic unlock | Generic chapter name | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compiling and types | 4, 8 | Numbers and math; Debugging | The heroine stumbles into the lab late at night. Ciel makes her compile by hand and read every error. Teaches main, std::cout, and why types matter for memory. |

### Confession
On the lab roof at dawn after an all-nighter, coat around her shoulders.

### Endings
• Good: Ciel lets the heroine contribute to their life's work, a game engine, and puts her name in the credits. Epilogue CG: both asleep on the keyboard as the build finishes.

• Bad: "Segmentation fault (core dumped)." The route crashes to a black screen, then Ciel walks past without recognizing her. Undefined behavior.
