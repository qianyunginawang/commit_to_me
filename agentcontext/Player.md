The heroine (player character)
A CS freshman who just moved into the dorms. Default name Grace (after Grace Hopper), and the player can rename her. She's never coded before, which is why every tutorial starts from zero. Her portrait shows only in the text box, so the player can project onto her. Optional choice at the start: "Why did you pick CS?" (games, money, curiosity, "I panicked"), which changes a few lines of dialogue.


Quack Overflow, the rubber duck
A yellow rubber duck the heroine gets at orientation, who turns out to be able to talk (only she can hear it). It's the classic otome best friend: it gives hints, reports affection levels ("Ciel's heart is at about 60%... or that's a memory leak"), and explains concepts when the player asks. Rubber duck debugging is a real technique, so the duck can also teach the player to explain their code out loud. Nickname: "Q".
