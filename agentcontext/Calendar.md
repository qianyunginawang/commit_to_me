# Calendar
The fall semester at Runtime University runs 14 weeks, from Monday, August 31 to Sunday, December 6 (the dates match 2026, so the weekdays line up). Week 1 starts with orientation and week 14 ends with finals and the end-of-term festival.

### How time works
- Each day has a Day slot (classes, office hours, the library) and a Night slot (hackathons, parties, the rooftop). Jay's day and night affection meters come from these.
- Weekends have no classes, so both slots are free for dates and events.
- Generic subject dates unlock in curriculum order. The pacing below is a suggestion: about 2 to 3 subjects a week, with the last one in finals week.

## Birthdays
- **Ciel Vector (C++), October 14, week 7.** It falls in the middle of midterms, and Ciel spends it alone in the lab, assuming nobody knows. A gift (black coffee at 3 a.m., a new keycap) is a big affection event, and a tsundere "I-it's not like I wanted a cake" scene.
- **Jay Ecma (JS/TS), December 4, week 14.** It's the day of the end-of-term festival, where Jay's site goes live and where Jay confesses. Night Jay throws a huge party in the group chat. Day Jay insists it's "just a normal day" and is secretly thrilled that the heroine remembered.
- **Julian Duke (Java), May 23.** It falls outside the semester. Julian formally observes a "half-birthday" on November 23 (week 13) "for scheduling reasons", with a printed agenda. The real birthday can show up in the epilogue.
- **Monty Pyne (Python), February 20.** It falls outside the semester. Save it for the epilogue or a post-game scene in spring.
- **Quack Overflow, orientation day (week 1).** Quack claims to be "one day old" and asks for cake every year.
- **The heroine:** the player could set it at the start. A default of December 9 (Grace Hopper's birthday) lands just after the semester, which works for an epilogue.

## Recurring events
- **Office hours:** Day Jay, weekday afternoons in Turing Hall (Day slot).
- **Hackathon nights:** Night Jay, every other weekend in the student union (Night slot), starting with the Day 256 Hackathon in week 2.
- **Student council meetings:** Julian, Mondays in the council room. Julian is always there early.
- **Gardening club:** Monty, Wednesday mornings in the greenhouse with Monty.
- **Tutoring center shift:** Monty, Thursday evenings at the tutoring center (Night slot). Monty brings tea and stays late for anyone who is stuck.
- **Game dev club:** Ciel, "unofficially" Friday night in the game dev club room, whenever Ciel is there.
- **Midnight lab session:** Ciel, Tuesday nights in Hopper Lab (Night slot). Ciel runs benchmarks and "happens" to have a spare seat and a second black coffee.
- **Coffee appreciation society:** Julian, Friday mornings at Café Cache.


## Route timing notes
- With the suggested pacing, the special finals unlock in this order: Jay's at subject 27 (week 11), Monty's at 29 (week 12), Julian's at 32 (week 13), and Ciel's at 33 (week 14).
- Every confession lands in week 14. Julian's is after the winter gala (Thu). Jay's and Monty's are at the end-of-term festival (Fri). Ciel's is on the CS building rooftop at dawn after the final all-nighter.
- Generic 24 (Linked lists) and 26 (Recursion) are set at the carnival, so they line up with Fright Night in weeks 9 and 10.
